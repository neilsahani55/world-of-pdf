Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your files stay on your device

All PDF processing on World of PDF happens locally in your web browser using JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to our servers — we do not have processing servers. We cannot see, store, share, or recover any document you work with.

What we store

The only thing this site stores is your theme preference, saved in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your browser and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Third-party resources

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the pdf-lib / PDF.js libraries from public CDNs (unpkg, cdnjs). These providers may log standard technical request data (such as your IP address) when your browser fetches those files, as described in their own privacy policies. Your documents are never sent to them.

Cookies, analytics, accounts

We do not use cookies, we do not run analytics or trackers, and there are no user accounts.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out via the contact page.
