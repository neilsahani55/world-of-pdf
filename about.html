About Us

World of PDF is a free collection of 48+ PDF tools that run entirely in your web browser. Merge, split, compress, convert, watermark, sign — all without creating an account or installing software.

What makes World of PDF different is simple: your files never leave your device. Every tool uses client-side JavaScript (powered by the open-source pdf-lib and PDF.js libraries) to process files locally in your browser. There is no upload server, no storage bucket, and no processing queue — which also means there is nothing for us to keep, leak, or sell.

Why we built this

Most online PDF services upload your documents to their servers and put limits, watermarks, or paywalls in front of basic operations. We believe simple document tasks should be free, fast, and private.
